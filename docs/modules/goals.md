# Module Scope: Savings Goals

## Purpose
This module helps a user define saving objectives such as travel, emergency fund, study, home purchase, or retirement planning.

## Business value
The product becomes more than tracking—it helps users plan future milestones and stay accountable to them.

## Included features
- Create financial goals with target amount and date
- Store saved amount progress
- Calculate goal completion percentage
- Estimate monthly savings required to meet the target
- Mark goals as active, completed, or cancelled

## Example use case
A user sets a goal to save ₹1,00,000 for a wedding in 12 months. The product estimates the monthly amount needed and flags whether the goal is on track or at risk.

## Why it matters for the client demo
Goals provide emotional and practical motivation. This module shows the product supports disciplined long-term financial planning, not just expense review.
